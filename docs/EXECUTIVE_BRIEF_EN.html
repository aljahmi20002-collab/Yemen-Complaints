Executive Brief

A concise management-level overview of the Yemen Complaints & Appeals Platform.

Docs Index

Business Value

  • Digital intake and tracking of citizen complaints and appeals worldwide.
  • Identity verification before submission using Email, SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
  • Structured routing, follow-up, SLA monitoring, and role-based operations.
  • AI-assisted intake and writing support for complaint advisors.

Core Modules

Complaint Intake
Web Forms, attachments, identity verification.
Case Management
Assignments, updates, workflow states, closure controls.
AI Assistance
ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, intake assistant, AI logs.
Reporting
Dashboards, number cards, script reports, print formats.

Go-Live Considerations

  • Configure production channels for SMS / WhatsApp / Telegram.
  • Enable AI only after provider keys, model validation, and usage policy approval.
  • Run role-based UAT and OTP verification tests.
  • Monitor AI logs, verification records, scheduler health, and outgoing communications.
